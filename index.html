About

I work at the intersection of algebraic structure, group theory, and the arithmetic of division algebras.

I am a lecturer in the Department of Mathematics and Informatics at the Ho Chi Minh City University of Education, Vietnam. I received my Ph.D. in Algebra and Number Theory from the University of Science, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City, in 2021.

My research concerns noncommutative algebra and its connections with linear groups, Leavitt path algebras, valued and graded division algebras, and Whitehead-type groups. I also work with students and collaborators on algebraic number theory, lattice methods, and computational experimentation.

I coordinate the HCMUE Algebra Seminar and take part in research-training activities that connect undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral students with current research problems.

Research

Research areas

The main themes below describe the mathematical settings in which I develop structural, group-theoretic, and computational questions.

Algebra

Division rings and skew linear groups

Subgroups of multiplicative groups and general linear groups over division rings, including maximal, locally solvable, and free subgroups.

Leavitt algebras

Leavitt path algebras

Lie ideals, derivations, unit groups, free subgroups, and structural questions for weighted and ordinary Leavitt path algebras.

Valuation theory

Valued and graded division algebras

Congruence theorems, exact sequences, scalar extension, and Whitehead-type invariants such as SK1 and TK1.

Structures

Lie and Jordan structures

Interactions between associative, Lie, and Jordan structures, with applications to ideals, derivations, and unit groups.

Number theory

Ideal lattices and well-rounded ideals

Collaborative research on ideal lattices in number fields, shortest vectors, Minkowski embeddings, and computationally guided conjectures.

K-theory

Whitehead-type groups

Stability and structural phenomena for unitary and graded invariants associated with division algebras and related algebraic systems.

University profile
Algebraic number theory

Well-rounded ideals in pure quartic fields

The project combines algebraic number theory, lattice methods, and computational experimentation with PARI/GP and SageMath.

The research-training cycle consists of reading the literature, computing examples, formulating conjectures, and proving selected cases.

Leavitt path algebras
